Smart Scale

A free plugin for Photoshop by Fe. Requires Photoshop 26.11 or later.

Scale layers, effects, corners, and strokes together, by percentage.

Smart Scale resizes your selected layers by the percentage you enter and keeps everything attached to them in proportion, so a resized button still looks like the same button.

What it scales

  • Layer size, from the center
  • Layer effects such as shadows, glows, strokes, and bevels
  • Rounded-rectangle corner radii, each corner on its own. Square corners stay square
  • Shape stroke widths, with dashes and gaps following along

How to use it

  • Select one or more layers or groups
  • Enter a percentage, for example 75, and choose Scale Selected
  • Use Advanced Settings to turn Resize Layers, Scale Effects, or Include Nested on or off

Good to know

Revert Last Scale is not an undo. It reverses your last scale by applying the opposite percentage. On pixel layers, scaling down and back up softens detail, so keep a copy of artwork you can't recreate.

  • Revert Last Scale only covers your most recent scale, in the same document
  • Revert Last Scale applies the opposite scale to the same layers, so use it right after scaling. If you've undone or changed the layers since, skip it

Privacy

Smart Scale makes no network requests and collects no data. It remembers only your three toggle settings, on your own computer. The Find Me links in the panel open my profiles in your browser, and only after you click and confirm.
